## real64 interface defect and repair

The first replay attempt could not start because the prototype accepted only
EXXCMP1 complex128, while an nspin=1 ABACUS run writes real64 C/V/D/H maps.
The repair dispatches one replay to `RI::Exx<double>` or
`RI::Exx<complex<double>>` from the common input scalar tag. Python compare and
project accept both same-type serial formats and preserve the input type.

TDD evidence:

- Python compare/project real64 tests first failed at the complex-only gate,
  then passed; the full suite is 68/68 on both the local worktree and server 66.
- The server-66 C++ real raw replay test first failed with
  `C snapshot must use complex128 scalars`, then matched direct
  `RI::Exx<double>` H, E, and D.full within `1e-13`.
- Server-66 CTest passes both EXX compression targets, 2/2.

## Why a fixed “second AO index” projection fails

The compact textbook expression

\[
K_{ik}=-\sum_{jl\mu\nu}C_{\mu ij}V_{\mu\nu}C^*_{\nu kl}D_{jl}
\]

has one clearly identified D-connected leg. Factoring
\(D=OO^\dagger\) and defining

\[
\bar C_{\mu iv}=\sum_j C_{\mu ij}O_{jv}
\]

is exact for that orientation and reduces the connected AO dimension from
\(N_{\rm AO}\) to \(N_{\rm occ}\). The phrase “second AO index” is not valid for
the complete LibRI implementation, however.

LibRI stores a three-center block as \(C_{a_0a_1a_2}\) and `cal_Hs` sums four
paths:

| LibRI label | D-connected C legs | free H legs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `a0b0_a1b1` | `a1`, `b1` | `a2`, `b2` |
| `a0b0_a1b2` | `a1`, `b2` | `a2`, `b1` |
| `a0b0_a2b1` | `a2`, `b1` | `a1`, `b2` |
| `a0b0_a2b2` | `a2`, `b2` | `a1`, `b1` |

Consequently, replacing the `a2` leg of every C block by one projected standard
C-map projects a contracted leg in some paths and a free output leg in others.
This explains the observed combination of an essentially exact exchange energy
and an incorrect full H: energy is a D-contracted scalar and can hide an output
projection that the SCF Hamiltonian cannot tolerate.

The negative-k result rules out a single Fourier-sign repair. The standard
C-map also ties the auxiliary center and the `a1` AO center to one outer atom
key. A global occupied projection of `a1` mixes AO atoms and therefore needs an
additional atom/momentum index; it cannot be represented losslessly by the
existing two-atom C-map.

## Revised method and stop decision

The GaAs submission remains stopped. The next exact gate must use either:

1. a label-aware LibRI contraction that factors the AO leg selected by each of
   the four paths while retaining the other AO leg; or
2. the periodic AO--occupied product

\[
B^{\mathbf k,\mathbf q}_{\mu i v}
=\sum_j C^{\mathbf k,\mathbf q}_{\mu i j}O^{\mathbf k-\mathbf q}_{jv},
\]

which explicitly carries both electron momentum and momentum transfer.

Only after this kernel reproduces the small-Si full H and E within `1e-10`
will the GaAs k444 PBE/EXX snapshot and TT/THC rank scan resume. An energy-only
occupied-occupied route remains a useful upper bound, but it is not a valid
replacement for the full SCF exchange operator.
